I am a material scientist and computational chemist seeking to use my skills to understand how to accelerate the development of materials for clean technology and decarbonization. I'm currently a postdoctoral research associate at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Iwnetim Abate. My research focuses on using machine learning, molecular simulations, robotics and automation to expedite the discovery process in heterogeneous ice nucleation, geological hydrogen production and carbon dioxide electrolysis.

Before MIT, I completed postdoctoral research in Prof. Curtis Berlinguette's lab where I worked on building self-driving laboratories for carbon dioxide electrolysis. I completed my PhD in Prof. Gren Patey's lab in the Department of Chemistry at the University of British Columbia, Canada. My doctoral research centres on investigating heterogeneous ice nucleation on various mineral surfaces through molecular simulations and machine learning. Prior to my Ph.D., I conducted my master's project under the guidance of Dr. Chandra Verma (NUS, Singapore) and Dr. Arun Venkatnathan (IISER, Pune). There, I focused on using molecular dynamics simulations to probe cryptic pockets in K-Ras and Dengue virus proteins. Additionally, during my undergraduate studies, I engaged in research projects spanning ionic liquids for polybenzimidazole fuel cell membranes and carbon dioxide capture.

Recent work

Closed-loop, human–AI-guided optimization of geological hydrogen production

Soni, A., Chiang, M., Evans, N., Bachu, S. K., Nielsen, J., Lan, H., Buonassisi, T., Abate, I. I. · ChemRxiv, 2026 (submitted)

Hydrogen can be stimulated directly from iron-bearing rock. Pairing human intuition with active learning over reaction conditions and rock composition, we reach yields up to 100× above the current state of the art — and identify which compositional features actually control the reaction.

Closed-loop, machine-learning-driven optimization of reactor yields in reactive carbon electrolyzers

Soni, A., Ma, S., Jewlal, A., et al., Berlinguette, C. · Nature Communications, accepted 2025 · also AI4Mat@NeurIPS 2025

A six-dimensional reaction space, optimized autonomously by AdaCarbon — a flexible automation platform of seven robots. The loop resolves the long-standing tension between carbon utilization and product selectivity in reactive carbon capture electrolysis.

Accelerated optimization of gas-diffusion electrodes for CO2 electrolyzers

Soni, A.,‡ Ma, S.,‡ Ocean, K., et al., Berlinguette, C. · Matter, 2025, 102519

The first automated zero-gap CO2 electrolyzer: robots fabricate, characterize and test gas-diffusion electrodes end to end, accelerating CO2 electrolysis research threefold.
